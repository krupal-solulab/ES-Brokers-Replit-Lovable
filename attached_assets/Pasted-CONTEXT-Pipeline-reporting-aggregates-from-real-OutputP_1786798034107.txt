EXACT LOGIC

inferred. Revenue = sum(bound_premium * commission_rate) per the config map, with
each figure carrying `provisional: true`. Both metrics keep FR-3's denominator +
low-volume annotation already implemented (MIN_RELIABLE_VOLUME).
