blanket coverage that handles this automatically.

Do not default to recommending the lower premium here - the "cheaper"
option may create more total cost/risk for THIS SPECIFIC account type
than the $3,500 premium difference reflects. Present both clearly with
this operational trade-off spelled out, and let the broker weigh in
based on what they know about how actively Brightline signs new
client contracts.
