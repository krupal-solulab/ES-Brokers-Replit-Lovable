TONE NOTES - Trigger 06

- This is the hardest tone case in this dataset, comparable to the
  MGA Non-Renewal Notice's difficulty level, for a different reason:
  the news itself (a bindable quote) is objectively GOOD relative to
  the alternative (no market at all, per Trigger 03's scenario type),
  but the PRICE is going to read as bad news if not framed correctly
- Explicitly connects the price to the specific loss history driving it
  - an agent (especially a newer relationship, per agent_relationship_
  tenure context) needs the "why" clearly stated, not just a number,
  or they'll assume the wholesaler is padding margin rather than
  passing through genuine market pricing
- Reframes the outcome relative to the realistic alternative ("one of
  the few markets willing to write this class at all... a real,
  bindable quote rather than another declination") - this is honest
  context, not spin, given Trigger 03's dataset already establishes
  that a genuine zero-market outcome is a real possibility in this
  vertical
- Because this is a newer agent relationship (1 year, per trigger
  data), the tone should be a notch more explanatory/patient than it
  might be with a longtime agent who already understands E&S pricing
  dynamics - this is the wholesale-specific analog to the MGA tone
  rules' relationship-tenure-awareness principle (TN-04/TN-07)
